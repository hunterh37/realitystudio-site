Privacy Policy

RealityStudio by Dicyanin Labs. Effective 2026-11-07.

Data collected

None. RealityStudio has no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

Camera, microphone and speech

Camera frames, face tracking, audio and speech recognition are processed on the device and are never uploaded. Clips are saved only to your Photos library when you choose to save them.

Location

The filter map uses your approximate location on the device to center the map. Location is not stored or sent to us.

Filter map

Map pins are read from Apple's CloudKit public database. Reading them sends no personal data to us.

Party mode

Party mode connects to nearby iPhones over the local network. Nothing is sent to a server.

Assistant

The scene assistant uses Apple's on-device model. Prompts do not leave the device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive payment details.

Children

RealityStudio does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
